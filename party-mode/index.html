Party Mode: Dumb Charades

Party Mode is a party game app with 10 games in one: Forehead Guess (dumb charades on your forehead), Imposter, Truth or Dare, Antakshari, Most Likely To, Never Have I Ever, Would You Rather, Bomb Pass, Don't Say It and Draw It. It has 84 decks of movies, songs, cricket and everyday desi life, and it works offline with no account.
