Party Mode Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Party Mode is a party game app published by HeeHaa. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign in, no analytics, and no server of its own. We do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What is stored on your phone

To work between sessions the app saves a small amount of data on your device only: your settings (round timer, haptics, Family mode, whether 18+ content is unlocked), the player names you last typed for each game, and a history of which cards you have already seen so they do not repeat. This data never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app uses the phone's motion sensors during Forehead Guess so you can tilt the phone to score or pass. Motion readings are used on the device in real time and are not stored or sent anywhere. The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Advertising

The current version of the app shows no ads. If ads are added in a future version, the ad provider may collect device identifiers (such as an advertising ID) and similar information to show and measure ads, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Age and 18+ content

The app has an optional 18+ setting that is off by default and is unlocked only after you confirm you are 18 or older. This confirmation is stored on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
